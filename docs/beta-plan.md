# Two-week beta plan

Status: not started. Generated fixtures and brief app runs cannot substitute for two weeks of voluntary use.

Before beta: close critical privacy/data-loss/unsafe-action defects; run the entire evidence matrix; establish supported-app paste outcomes; test denied permissions and recovery; publish exact network/signature behavior; obtain a signed/notarized candidate through human-controlled credentials.

Use at least an M1 MacBook Air with 8 GB, a newer Apple silicon Mac, an external display, macOS 14 and each declared newer supported OS. Record hardware, OS/app versions, keyboard layout, scaling, display, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion/Increase Contrast and update-consent state. Use a fixture of 200 recent and 1,000 saved items with documented text/image proportions and sizes. Keep private clipboard content out of logs/screenshots/reports.

Day 1: observe a new participant retrieving an earlier synthetic copy within three minutes; record actual time and difficulty. Explain capture, retention, exclusions, manual fallback and optional Accessibility consent. Permission grants are voluntary and human-controlled.

Days 2–13: voluntary daily use. Collect plain reports of reliability, mistaken assumptions, recovery issues, shortcut conflicts and whether saved groups help a real weekly workflow. No installed analytics or automatic upload. Pause/revocation is supported at any time.

Day 14: collect voluntary feedback, record actual duration and participating environments, reproduce reported issues with synthetic fixtures, rerun affected checks. Do not infer adoption or reliability from absent reports.

Release blockers: any wrong-target dispatch; unresolved lost saved content; plaintext artifacts; collection during pause/denial; unvalidated release claims; paste outcomes below98%; failed performance budget on the reference fixture; incomplete keyboard/VoiceOver workflows. Keep unavailable-device checks open with the exact required environment.
