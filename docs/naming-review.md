# Winnel naming

The product name is **Winnel by Made Ordinary**. The generic word “stack” describes a saved collection within the app.

The name is settled for implementation. Final trademark review in intended markets and distribution-name checks remain release tasks. This document is not legal clearance, a domain reservation or an App Store name reservation.

Product requirements live in [PRD.md](../PRD.md). Naming does not change product scope, storage identifiers or technical acceptance requirements.
